# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want BG Eraser to be a friendly and respectful project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful.
- Accept constructive feedback and give it politely.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam or other disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who do.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's "Report content" feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or contact the maintainer, [@ErwannRobin](https://github.com/ErwannRobin), through GitHub. Reports are handled in private.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to people representing the project in public.
